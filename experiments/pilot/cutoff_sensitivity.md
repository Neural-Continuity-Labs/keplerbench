## Does the ordering persist?

The ordering persists at every cutoff from 1592 to 1595: the unfitted oracle has the lowest held-out median (3.31 to 4.82 arcmin), and the fitted ellipse (6.90 to 27.40 arcmin) beats every flexible fit held out by a factor of at least 11. At every cutoff the ellipse's held-out interval lies entirely below every flexible fit's interval, while the Gaussian process remains the best in-sample fit (only marginally: 1.77 against 1.78 for the ellipse at 1592), so the in-sample and held-out rankings disagree throughout. The ellipse's own held-out median is not stable across cutoffs, lowest at 1592 and highest at 1593, and the order among the flexible fits changes from cutoff to cutoff, so the two headline relations are robust but the full ranking is not. The caveat is that the four held-out windows are nested rather than independent (each later cutoff's window is a subset of the earlier ones, and all of them contain the 1595, 1596 and 1600 epochs), the 1594 campaign has only 5 epochs, so the 1594 and 1595 cutoffs differ by those 5 training epochs alone, and the 1600 tail has 7 epochs at every cutoff, below the 10-epoch minimum for a quoted median.
